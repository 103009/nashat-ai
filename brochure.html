The problem

Bahrain's summer is a fitness problem

38°C → 55°C

38 °C air with 60% humidity feels like 55 °C. Sweat can't cool you down.

  • From June to September it is unsafe to exercise outside for much of the day. Bahrain already bans outdoor work at midday in summer.
  • So people train indoors, alone. Nobody corrects shallow squats or sagging push-ups, and bad form can cause injury.
  • It is hard to know when it is safe to train outside.

Who it affects

Students and young people who want to stay active, people exercising while fasting in Ramadan, and beginners who can't afford a trainer.

How the AI works

From data to feedback

1

Data

Many photos of people, with their joints marked by hand.

2

Training

A neural network learns to find joints by fixing its mistakes millions of times (done by Google).

3

Prediction

On your phone, it finds 33 body points in every video frame. No video is uploaded.

4

Our rules

Our code turns the points into angles and a depth score, then counts reps and checks form.

How to use it

  1. Check the heat. Live Manama weather gives a safe plan and the coolest time to train, with a Ramadan mode.

  2. Prop up your phone 2–3 m away and pick squats, push-ups or jumping jacks.

  3. Train. The AI counts every rep, says "go lower" or "keep your chest up", and times your rest.

Works at home: phone on the floor, side-on

What AI should NOT do

Diagnose injuries, decide alone that it is safe to train, or store videos of people.

Ethics & impact

Bias: the AI may work less well for some body types or loose clothing like thobes and abayas. Our Test Lab records accuracy by clothing and lighting so the rules can be tuned.

Privacy: the AI runs in your browser. Video is never uploaded or saved, and there are no accounts.

Safety: heat limits, rest and water reminders, and "stop if dizzy" warnings. Guidance, not medical advice.

Jobs: for times when no coach is around, not a replacement for trainers.

Environment: a small 6 MB model on your device, no server running all the time.
