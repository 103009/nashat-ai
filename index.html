The problem

Summer heat in Bahrain

From June to September it is often too hot and humid to exercise outside safely in the middle of the day. Many people don't know when it is safe and when it is not.

The problem

Training alone at home

When it is too hot outside, people train indoors with no coach. Bad form, like shallow squats or sagging push-ups, wastes effort and can cause injury.

Our AI solution

A coach in your phone

Computer vision finds 33 points on your body in every video frame. Nashat turns them into joint angles to count reps and give feedback by voice, live.

How to use it
