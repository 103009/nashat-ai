How Nashat works

From camera to coach in four steps

01

Point your camera

Prop up your phone 2–3 m away. Each video frame goes straight to the AI model, on your phone.

02

AI finds 33 points

A neural network trained on many labelled photos of people predicts where your joints are, many times a second.

03

Measure the angles

Our code turns the points into joint angles. Knee below 100°? That's full depth. Back up past 160°? One rep.

04

Coach in real time

You get the rep count, form tips by voice, rest timers, and a heat limit that stops you before it gets unsafe.
