Ethics & impact

Privacy in the app

  • The AI model is part of this website and runs on your device.
  • Camera video is never uploaded, recorded or saved.
  • No accounts, names or photos. Test results use codes like "T07" and stay in this browser.
  • The only thing sent over the internet is a weather request for Manama's location (not yours).

Try it on your phone

Scan the code to open Nashat. Allow camera access when asked.

Before printing, test the QR code with at least two different phones.
